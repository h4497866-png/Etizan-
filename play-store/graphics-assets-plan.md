# SKY RUSH — Google Play graphics

## Approved store direction
- Free arcade game with virtual points only.
- No deposits, cash withdrawals, real-money prizes, or in-app purchases in the current release.
- Store listing copy is maintained in `store-listing-draft.md`.

## Artwork prepared for review
The artwork files are prepared locally for review and upload:
- App icon: `sky-rush-icon-512.png` — 512 × 512 PNG, opaque RGBA.
- Feature graphic: `sky-rush-feature-graphic-1024x500.jpg` — 1024 × 500 JPEG.

The icon and feature graphic are concept artwork, not screenshots of gameplay. The feature graphic intentionally avoids claims about features that have not been verified in the current build.

## Still required before store submission
- Review and approve the icon and feature graphic.
- Capture at least two genuine screenshots from the tested final Android build; do not use generated artwork as screenshots.
- Confirm the support email and publish a public privacy-policy URL after the advertising SDK/data practices are decided.
- Complete Play Console declarations, including ads, content rating, target audience, and Data safety.
- For a new personal developer account, complete the required closed test before applying for production access.
