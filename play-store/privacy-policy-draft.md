## 1. Current app operation

The current SKY RUSH Android build is an offline arcade game. It does not require an account, and game progress and virtual-point statistics are stored locally on the user's device using local browser storage inside the app. The current reviewed build does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated account/server service.

## 2. Information stored on the device

The game stores gameplay state such as the virtual-points balance, completed rounds, wins, losses, and round history locally so that progress can be retained on the same device. This information is not sent to a SKY RUSH server by the current reviewed build.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored progress.

## 3. Advertising and third-party services

Advertising is planned for a future version, but no advertising SDK is included in the currently reviewed build. Before an advertising SDK is added, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated to identify the provider, the categories of data it may collect or share, the purposes of processing, and any available user controls. Do not treat this draft as the final disclosure for an ad-supported release.

## 4. Children's privacy

SKY RUSH is not currently declared as a child-directed app. The final target-audience selection will be made in Google Play Console. If the intended audience changes, the app's design, SDK choices, disclosures, and policy will be reviewed against the applicable requirements.

## 5. Data security and retention

The current game data is stored locally on the device. The developer does not operate a cloud account or game-progress server for the current build. Users can remove locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## 6. Changes to this policy

This policy must be revised whenever the app adds advertising, analytics, online services, account features, or any other feature that changes data collection or sharing. The effective policy published for users must accurately describe the version they install.
