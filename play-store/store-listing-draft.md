## English listing

### Short description
Fly, dodge bombs, and choose the right moment to end each round.

### Full description
Take off with SKY RUSH, a fast-paced arcade game that challenges your focus and timing.

Steer your plane left and right to avoid bombs while the round multiplier rises. Decide when to end the round before the plane crashes, collect virtual points, and progress through game levels.

Features:
- Steer the plane during each round.
- Dodge incoming bombs.
- Follow the round multiplier and potential virtual points.
- Track your points balance, round history, and statistics.
- Progress through multiple levels.
- Play without creating an account.

Important:
SKY RUSH uses virtual in-game points for entertainment only. There are no real-money deposits, withdrawals, transfers, or real-world prizes. Points cannot be exchanged for money or goods. Rounds include random outcomes.
